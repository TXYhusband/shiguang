Study Companion adapts the cat's walking/running sequence from src/panel/pets/cat.ts and the horizontal boundary movement from src/panel/states.ts into electron/pet-behavior.mjs. It replaces frame counters with elapsed-time transitions, removes unsupported wall climbing, and adds food, petting, playing and sleep states. Electron window movement uses explicit desktop work-area bounds.

Artwork is Study Companion's original SVG cat, food, hearts and ball. The upstream Catset sprites and other third-party art are NOT included. This notice and the MIT license are included in the built application and accessible from Preferences.

The full shallow clone used for inspection is kept locally at .cache/upstream/vscode-pets (not included in application packages).
